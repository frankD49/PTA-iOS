Community Guidelines

Precious Tots Academy (PTA)
Effective/version date: 15 August 2026

PTA is a school community. Be respectful, protective of children, and mindful that group-chat content is visible to other approved members.

Never post

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or hateful content.
  • Sexual, exploitative, or grooming content.
  • A child’s information or image without authorization.
  • Spam, scams, or impersonation.
  • Illegal content or instructions facilitating harm.
  • Confidential or unauthorized school information.

Reporting and blocking

Use the clearly labelled message menu to report a message, report a user, or block a user. Blocking hides that person’s messages for you and may make a shared conversation less complete. Reports are private from the reported user.

Enforcement

Reported content may be hidden or removed and accounts may be warned, suspended, or deleted. Standard reports are targeted for review within 48 hours. Child-safety concerns are escalated immediately to the designated safeguarding lead and, where appropriate, relevant authorities. Evidence access is limited to authorized administrators and retained according to the Privacy Policy.
