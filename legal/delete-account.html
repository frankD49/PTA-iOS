Delete Your PTA Account

Precious Tots Academy (PTA)
Effective/version date: 15 August 2026

This page must be hosted on the academy’s public HTTPS website and its final URL entered in Google Play Console and App Store Connect.

In-app request

Sign in to the PTA app, open Menu → Account & Legal → Delete account, authenticate using your device security, and confirm. Access is revoked immediately. Permanent deletion is scheduled after 14 days.

If you cannot access the app

Email precioustotsacademy@outlook.com from the account email. PTA will verify account ownership and may request limited additional confirmation. Do not send passwords or biometric information.

What happens

The Supabase Auth account, name, email, and solely owned uploads are deleted. Group-chat sender identity is anonymized as “Deleted member.” Minimal moderation and consent evidence may be retained for safeguarding or legal needs for up to 24 months. Provider logs/backups normally expire within 90 days.

Timing and recovery

Requests are acknowledged immediately and completed after the 14-day recovery window. Contact support from the registered email during that window to request cancellation.
